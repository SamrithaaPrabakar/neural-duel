NEURAL DUEL

An opponent that learns to read you, one round at a time.

ABOUT THE AI

The CPU fighter is controlled by tabular Q-learning, not a fixed script.

Every ~220ms it reads a discretized state — distance to you, both health bands, grounded/airborne, your current action — and picks a move.

Landing a hit rewards it (+10). Taking one punishes it (-10). Winning or losing a round gives a large terminal reward (±50). It updates its Q-table live, from your actual fights — no pre-training.

Its sprite glitches early on because it's still exploring at random (high ε). As ε decays and it commits to what works, the glitch clears — you can watch it learn.
